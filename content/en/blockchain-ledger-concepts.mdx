# Blockchain & Ledgers: The Future of Transparency

Blockchain is more than cryptocurrency. It is a new approach to managing a **decentralized ledger**.

## 1. The technical essence
- **Immutability:** Once data is written into a block and its hash is computed, it is practically impossible to change.
- **Consensus:** How do thousands of nodes agree on the same data? (PoW, PoS).
- **Smart Contracts:** Code that executes itself on the blockchain when certain conditions are met.

## 2. Real-world applications
- **Supply Chain:** Tracing the origin of goods.
- **Finance (DeFi):** Removing intermediaries from financial transactions.
- **Identity Management:** Managing personal identity securely.

> *"Blockchain combines modern cryptography with game theory to create trust in a decentralized environment."*
