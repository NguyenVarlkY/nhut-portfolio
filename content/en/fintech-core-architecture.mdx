# Financial System Architecture (Fintech Core)

In financial systems, **consistency** matters more than anything. A single failed money transaction can lead to losses in the billions.

## 1. ACID - the foundation of a Fintech core
Only systems that follow these principles are fit to serve as core banking:
- **Atomicity:** Either everything succeeds, or nothing happens.
- **Consistency:** Data is always in a valid state.
- **Isolation:** Concurrent transactions do not affect each other.
- **Durability:** Once data is committed, it is never lost.

## 2. The ledger system
This is the heart of every financial application.
- **Double-Entry Bookkeeping:** Every entry is recorded as a debit and a credit, and total debits always equal total credits.
- **Audit Log:** Every change must leave a trace (an immutable audit trail).

## 3. Technical challenges
- **Concurrency:** How do you prevent "double spending" when millions of transactions happen at once? (Use optimistic or pessimistic locking at the database level.)
- **ISO 8583:** The standard for financial card transactions.
- **Security:** Encryption in transit and at rest, and hardware security modules (HSM) for keys.
