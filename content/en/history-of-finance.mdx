# From Seashells to Crypto: A History of Finance

Understanding the history of money is the key to understanding why we build Fintech systems the way we do today.

## 1. The evolution of value
- **Barter:** Low efficiency.
- **Commodity money (seashells, salt, gold):** Easy to store and exchange.
- **Fiat money:** Based on trust in the government.

## 2. The banking revolution
The rise of the banking system (ledgers, transfers) created trust, which allowed global trade to flourish.

## 3. The digital era
The fusion of finance and technology (Fintech) is disrupting traditional banking models, putting users at the center with faster, cheaper, and more transparent applications.

> *"History doesn't repeat itself, but it often rhymes. Understanding the past helps us avoid repeating old mistakes in financial architecture."*
